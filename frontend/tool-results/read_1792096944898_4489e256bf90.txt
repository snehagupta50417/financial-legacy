   562→   562→- What remains uncertain.
   563→   563→- Why the next action is recommended.

   749→   749→## How to make the hackathon demo stand out
   750→   750→
   751→   751→My recommendation is to demo one end-to-end case extremely well, rather than trying to demonstrate every persona equally.
   752→   752→
   753→   753→Step 1 — Start with a real human problem
   754→   754→
   755→   755→Enter: “My father passed away. I found an old bank statement and insurance policy, but I don't know what he owned or how to claim it.”
   756→   756→
   757→   757→Step 2 — Show AI document intelligence
   758→   758→
   759→   759→Upload synthetic documents. Show extracted institution names, possible asset references, evidence excerpts, confidence, and user verification.
   760→   760→
   761→   761→Step 3 — Show the journey engine
   762→   762→
   763→   763→The agent generates separate workflows for each institution, identifies missing documents, and prioritises the next best action.
   764→   764→
   765→   765→Step 4 — Take a real action
   766→   766→
   767→   767→Open RBI UDGAM or the relevant official insurance portal from within the task. Explain exactly what the user must do there.
   768→   768→
   769→   769→Step 5 — Return and resume
   770→   770→
   771→   771→Mark the external step as completed, upload a fictional acknowledgement, and show the case timeline and next action updating automatically.
   772→   772→
   773→   773→The strongest differentiator: the AI doesn't just tell someone where to look. It turns scattered records into an evidence-backed plan, coordinates multiple workflows, and helps the family continue until each case reaches a documented outcome.
   774→   774→
   775→   775→One important technical constraint: the public portals above are real, but that does not mean their underlying search systems expose APIs. Build the live AI and database functionality first, then add direct institutional API integrations only where access is actually available.
